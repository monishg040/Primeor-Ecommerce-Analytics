# 🛒 Primeor E-commerce Analytics

## 📌 Project Overview

Primeor E-commerce Analytics is a data analytics and business intelligence project focused on analyzing e-commerce sales, profit, customers, products, markets, regions, categories, shipping modes, discounts, and sales trends.

The project uses **Excel, SQL, and Power BI** to transform e-commerce data into meaningful business insights and interactive visualizations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance
- Identify high-performing markets and regions
- Analyze category and sub-category performance
- Identify the most profitable products
- Analyze customer sales performance
- Study shipping mode usage
- Analyze discount patterns
- Identify negative-profit transactions
- Analyze monthly sales trends
- Build dashboards to support business analysis and decision-making

---

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

## 🌍 Market Performance

**APAC** generates the highest revenue at approximately **$3.58M**.

## 🌎 Regional Sales

**Central** generates the highest sales at approximately **$2.82M**.

## 💰 Category Profitability

**Technology** has the highest average profit at approximately **$65.42**.

Technology also generates the highest total profit among the three major categories.

## 🪑 Furniture

Furniture has the lowest total profit at approximately **$0.29M** and the highest average discount at approximately **16.8%**.

## 📦 Shipping

**Standard Class** is the most commonly used shipping mode with **30,764 orders**.

## 📱 Sub-Category

**Phones** generate the highest sub-category sales at approximately **$1.71M**.

## 🏆 Most Profitable Product

**Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** generated the highest total profit at approximately **$25.2K**.

## 👤 Customer Sales

**Tom Ashbrook** is the top customer by total sales at approximately **$40.5K**.

## 📉 Negative Profit

The analysis identified **12,538 records with negative profit**.

## 📅 Sales Trend

The monthly sales analysis covers **48 months from 2011 to 2014**, allowing sales performance to be analyzed over time.
